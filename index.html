How I workWith a new government client

  1. Listen & MapMandate, timeline, sensitivities, and every stakeholder: departments, partners, field teams, communities.
  2. TranslatePolicy into audience-specific messages, in the right language and register, with the proof points and the lines to avoid.
  3. BuildOne campaign thread across press, social, outdoor and on-ground, with approvals and a crisis protocol agreed in advance.
  4. Run & RespondReal-time coordination, media management and onsite execution, with risks flagged early.
  5. Review & ReportWhat reached whom, reported to the client in plain language, then carried into the next phase.

Principles

About and writing

For most of my career, I described myself as a communications professional. Looking back, I think I was solving a different problem.

Whether I was reporting from the ground, designing public campaigns with government institutions, writing proposals for mission-driven organisations, or helping museums engage communities, the work was never just about communication. It was about helping people make sense of complexity. The hardest problems are rarely communication problems alone; they're problems of alignment, trust and clarity.

Today I work at the intersection of strategic communication, stakeholder engagement, project management and AI-enabled knowledge work. Alongside my consulting, I'm building Uncooked Club, a community exploring originality, creativity and what it means to remain deeply human in the age of AI.
